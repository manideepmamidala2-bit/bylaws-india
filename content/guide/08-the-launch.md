## Slide summary

The launch roadmap runs from the quiet pilot and partner MoUs in December 2026 to the public launch and first student workshop in Bengaluru in the last week of January 2027, followed by the May–July "Verify Bengaluru" internship cohort.

## Presenter notes

Here is our launch timeline from late November 2026 through December 2027. Between 30 November and 6 December 2026, the trust round and pilot work merge, the partner kit is drafted, the launch host college is chosen, and first contact begins with the national students' architecture association about a student design trophy. From 7 to 20 December, Zenodo DOIs are switched on, the engine beta starts, and we run a quiet pilot with a handful of architects and faculty. After fixes, a demo video, and faculty sessions in early January, the public launch takes place in Bengaluru in the last week of January 2027 alongside the first student workshop, leading into the May–July "Verify Bengaluru" internship cohort and the December 2027 one-year report.

### Launch timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **30 Nov – 6 Dec 2026** | Trust round merged; pilot work running; partner kit drafted; launch host college chosen; first contact with the national students' architecture association about a trophy |
| **7 – 20 Dec** | Pilot work merged; Zenodo DOIs on; engine beta started; quiet pilot with a handful of architects and faculty |
| **21 Dec – 4 Jan 2027** | Fixes from the quiet pilot; demo video; press kit |
| **5 – 16 Jan** | Engine beta ready; faculty sessions at the host college; first MoUs finalised |
| **Last week of Jan** | Public launch in Bengaluru; first student workshop the same week |
| **Feb – Mar** | Workshops at more colleges; first MoUs signed in Karnataka and other states; monthly "What changed" note begins |
| **Apr – mid May** | Exam season: workshops pause; internship cohort recruited |
| **May – Jul** | "Verify Bengaluru" internship cohort; release with named student credits |
| **Aug – Oct** | Second workshop season; a steward college starts its state's rules |
| **Dec 2027** | One-year report |

### What happens at the launch, workshops and internships

- **At the public launch (last week of January 2027):** Live five-minute demonstration of the Bengaluru pilot (`{{documents_in_force}}` in-force documents, `{{provisions}}` provisions, `{{rules_as_data}}` rules as data, `{{wards}}` wards), presentation of the host college MoU, and the first one-day student verification workshop in the same week.
- **Semester workshops & internships:** Each partner college hosts a one-day hands-on workshop and a two-hour faculty session during the semester, followed by the May–July **"Verify Bengaluru"** 60 to 120-hour supervised internship cohort where students verify provisions, parameter cells, and map sheets for named credit in the summer release.
