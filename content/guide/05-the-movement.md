## Slide summary

Bylaw One is organised around four roles — State stewards, Contributors, Reviewers, and Maintainers — and a planned three-tier pack architecture (national, state, and city) that records whether each higher rule is adopted, adopted with changes, or reference only.

## Presenter notes

No single team can curate and verify the building rules of every Indian city alone. Bylaw One is organised as a collective movement across four clear roles: State stewards (colleges that curate their state's rules, verify records, and teach with them), Contributors (anyone proposing sources, corrections, or cities), Reviewers (faculty and practitioners who sign off verification), and Maintainers (the core team that runs the engine, data format, and checks). Bylaw One is run as a brand under the owner's organisation, and all released work stays open regardless. To scale across India without duplicating text, we have designed a planned layered pack architecture — national, state, and city — where shared text is stored once and each city pack records whether a state or national model rule was adopted, adopted with changes, or is reference only.

## Reading version

### How the work is organised

- **State stewards:** colleges that curate their state's rules, verify records and teach with them.
- **Contributors:** anyone proposing sources, corrections or cities.
- **Reviewers:** faculty and practitioners who sign off verification.
- **Maintainers:** the core team that runs the engine, data format and checks.
- Bylaw One is run as a brand under **{{organisation_name}}**; released work stays open regardless (code under Apache-2.0, data under CC BY 4.0).

### Layered packs (planned)

Rules will be published as downloadable packs (**planned**): **national**, **state** and **city**. Shared text is stored once. Each layer records how it relates to the one above:

1. **Adopted:** A state or city notification brings the higher-tier provision into force verbatim.
2. **Adopted with changes:** A state or city notification adopts a model rule (such as Bye-law 1.4 from the 2017 notification or a corporation-specific Schedule-IV table) with local substitutions or conditions.
3. **Reference only:** A national model or standard (such as the National Building Code 2016 or the 2017 Model Building Bye-laws issued under the Municipalities Act 1964) is recorded for cross-reference because a national or state model only applies where a state or city adopted it.

A city pack shows each rule's complete trail from national or state authority down to the municipal corporation.
