## Presenter notes

Welcome to Bylaw One. Look at any building plot in Bengaluru: the rules that govern it are not in a single book. They sit across {{documents_in_force}} in-force acts, building bye-laws, master plan zoning regulations, and gazette amendments issued by {{issuing_bodies}} bodies over twenty-two years. Bylaw One joins those official publications into one open, cited, machine-readable code. Today the working pilot covers {{provisions}} structured provisions, {{rules_as_data}} governing parameter rules as data, {{corporations}} City Corporations, {{wards}} official wards, and {{active_zones}} georeferenced land-use zone polygons across {{processed_sheets}} ({{map_coverage_pct}} of Greater Bengaluru), verified by {{checks_per_build}} automatic checks on every build.

## Reading version

**Bengaluru's building rules. One code.** Every building in Bengaluru is governed by {{documents_in_force}} in-force instruments from {{issuing_bodies}} statutory bodies — municipal building bye-laws, master plan zoning regulations, dated gazette amendments, corporation notifications, and state Acts — current as of **{{current_as_of}}**.

Bylaw One joins those scattered official PDFs into one readable, cited, machine-readable building code. Nothing inside the legal text is rewritten or invented; every provision and table row links directly to the exact page of the official government PDF and its Internet Archive snapshot.
