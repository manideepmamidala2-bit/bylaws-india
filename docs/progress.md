3. **Unmapped amending fragments and slice eviction on subject pages**: `app/j/[id]/topics/[topic]/page.tsx` collected topic annotations from corporation amendment files (`*-bbl-amend-2026-05`) and sorted `isNodeCorporationSpecific` ahead of shared provisions before slicing `.slice(0, 6)` / `.slice(0, 7)`, causing `7` subjects (`approvals-and-certificates`, `basements-and-stilts`, `definitions`, `deviations-and-compounding`, `far`, `fire-safety`, `ground-coverage`, `height`, `land-use`, `plot-size-and-frontage`, `projections-and-open-spaces`, `setbacks` — `12` subject pages total across the 5 corporations = `60` subject-corporation pairs) to differ in provision ordering or slice membership between the city view and corporation views.
