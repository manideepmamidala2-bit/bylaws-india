# Scope: Building Rules for Bengaluru South City Corporation

## 1. The Inclusion Test

Every candidate statute, rule, regulation, bye-law, notification, circular, or map is evaluated against one inclusion test:

> **Does it decide what can be built on a plot, or what a building must comply with to be approved and occupied?**

## 2. Three Scope Tiers

| Tier | What | Treatment |
|------|------|-----------|
| **Core** | Building Bye-laws 2003 and every amendment (including the 2026 Bengaluru South City Corporation amendment); RMP 2015 Zoning Regulations and every amendment; RMP 2015 land-use maps for Bengaluru South | Structured completely |
| **Supporting** | Building-relevant provisions of other instruments: fire NOC for buildings of 21 m and above; TDR rules as they affect usable FAR; occupancy certificate exemption orders; metro influence zone; lake buffers once their legal status is verified; anything else that passes the inclusion test | Only the relevant provisions structured; linked to the full instrument |
| **Register only** | Whole Acts beyond their building provisions; procedural circulars; National Building Code (copyrighted by BIS) | Listed with links; not structured |

## 3. Language & Gaps Policy

- **English only**: A building instrument that exists only in Kannada is listed in the source register with a link and a declared gap in `declarations.json`, and is not structured.
- **No synthetic or reconstructed sources (`Rule R16`)**: If an instrument or map sheet cannot be obtained from an authentic source, it is recorded in the register with `acquisition_status: not_found` and declared as a gap.
- **Excluded materials**: Withdrawn or un-notified master plan drafts (such as the withdrawn RMP 2031 draft and RMP 2041 preparatory materials) are excluded from the in-force dataset.
