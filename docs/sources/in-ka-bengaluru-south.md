## 2. Core Regulatory Framework

Building construction and land development in Bengaluru South City Corporation are governed by two primary statutory instruments and their chronological amendments:

1. **Municipal Building Bye-Laws (`layer: municipal`)**:
   - **`bbl-2003`** — *BANGALORE MAHANAGARA PALIKE BUILDING BYE-LAWS 2003* (`Order No.UDD/223/MNU/2001`, dated `21-02-2004`, effective `05-06-2004`).
   - **Legal Continuity**: Notified under Section 428 of the *Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976*, and continued in force under Section 375(2)(b) of the *Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike Act, 2020* and Section 370 of the *Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024* until superseded by new corporation bye-laws. Actively listed on the official GBA/BBMP Building Plan Approval System (`https://bpas.bbmpgov.in/BPAMSClient4/CommonForms/Circulars.aspx`).
   - **2026 Draft Amendment (`bscc-bbl-amend-2026`)**: On `04-03-2026`, a draft notification (*Bengaluru South City Corporation Building (Amendment) Bye-laws 2026*, `bscc-10125.pdf`) was published inviting objections within 30 days. Direct download from `data.opencity.in` is currently blocked by Cloudflare Turnstile (`HTTP 403`) with no Wayback snapshot; it is recorded in the register with `legal_status: "draft"` and `acquisition_status: "not_found"` per Rule R16.

2. **State Model Building Bye-Laws Reference (`layer: state_rules`)**:
   - **`ka-model-bbl-2017`** — *Karnataka Municipalities Model Building Bye-Laws, 2017* (`No. UDD 14 TTP 2017 (P-4)`, dated `28-10-2017`). Published on the GBA/BBMP Building Plan Approval System (`bpas.bbmpgov.in/BPAMSClient4/CommonForms/Circulars.aspx`) under *Bye laws and Zoning Regulations* as a state reference document (`role: reference`).

3. **Master Plan Zoning Regulations & Amendments (`layer: planning`)**:
   - **`rmp2015-zr`** — *Revised Master Plan 2015 BANGALORE 2007 Volume – III ZONING OF LANDUSE AND REGULATIONS* (`G.O. No UDD 540 BEM AA SE 2004`, dated `22-06-2007` / `25-06-2007`).
   - **`rmp2015-zr-amend-2009-03`** — *Notification No.UDD 93 MNJ 2008* dated `02-03-2009` (amending Clause `4.10 Traffic and Transportation (T&T)` and `Table 19`, plus BMRCL FAR 4.0 provision).
   - **`rmp2015-zr-amend-2015-03`** — *Notification No: UDD 105 MNJ 2008* dated `20-03-2015`, published in *Karnataka Gazette Part-II No. 230* dated `23-03-2015` (withdrawing the `11-12-2014` notification pursuant to High Court W.P. 2661/2015 and amending permissible commercial/ancillary uses in Residential Main `4.1.2` and Residential Mixed `4.2.2` across Rings I, II, and III).
   - **`rmp2015-zr-amend-2018-09`** — *Zoning Regulations of Bengaluru of the Revised Master Plan-2015 (Amendment) Regulations 2018* (`NO. UDD 112 MNJ 2015`, dated `29-09-2018`, *Karnataka Gazette Extraordinary Part-IVA No. 1376*), inserting `3.4(vi) Impact Zone Premium FAR`, omitting `3.16(ix)`, and adding `Appendix-IX`.
   - **`rmp2015-zr-amend-2019-06`** — *Zoning Regulations of Bengaluru of the Revised Master Plan-2015 (Amendment) Regulations, 2019* (`No. UDD 255 MNJ 2018`, dated `19-06-2019`, published `20-06-2019` in *Karnataka Extraordinary Gazette Part-IVA No. 483*), amending definitions (`Apartment Building`, `Non residential Development Plan`), `3.1` setback exemptions, `3.4` FAR exclusions `(i)`–`(xvi)`, `3.4-A Height of Building`, `3.6(i)`, `3.9(ix)`, Chapter 6, Chapter 7 (`Tables 20–22`), and Chapter 8 (`Table 23` parking for star hotels and office buildings).
   - **`rmp2015-zr-comp-2024-10-kn`** — *Revising the amendments in the regulations in BBMP revised Mahayojane 2015* (`BBMP/TP/2024-25`, dated `19-10-2024`, `KM_266_160102035827-pages-deleted_29102024.pdf`). Kannada-only 36-page compilation (`languages: ["kn"]`); acquired and declared as a language gap per `docs/plans/pr-series/00-overview.md`.
   - **`rmp2015-zr-amend-2025-02`** — *Notification No. UDD 78 MNJ 2024 (E)* dated `21-02-2025` (`NOTIFICATION-1` and `NOTIFICATION-II`, *Karnataka Special Gazette Part-IVA*), introducing city-wide Premium FAR tiers (20% / 40% / 60% by road width) and fee/TDR rules.
   - **`rmp2015-zr-amend-2025-07`** — *Gazette Notification (July 2025) - Bengaluru Master Plan 2015 Zoning Regulations Amendments* (`d7b00660-a3bc-49cf-b35a-3d70283ace0a.pdf`). Recorded with `acquisition_status: "not_found"` per Rule R16 due to Cloudflare Turnstile challenge (`HTTP 403`) on `data.opencity.in` and absence of a Wayback snapshot.
   - **`rmp2015-zr-amend-2026-01`** — *Notification No. UDD 235 MNJ 2025(E)* dated `05-01-2026` (*Zonal Regulations of Revised Master Plan-2015 of the Local Planning Area of Bengaluru and Greater Bengaluru (Amendment) Regulations, 2025*), substituting `Table 8` (setbacks for buildings up to 12.0m height / 15.0m with stilt on plots $\le$ 250 sq.m), amending `3.9` (basements) and `3.10` (ramps and car lifts), and revising Ring I–III apartment plot thresholds, `Table 15` (Mutation Corridors), and `Table 16` (Industrial I).
