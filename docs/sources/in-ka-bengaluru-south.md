# Source Register: Bengaluru South City Corporation (`in-ka-bengaluru-south`)

**Jurisdiction**: Bengaluru South City Corporation (`in-ka-bengaluru-south`, Greater Bengaluru Authority)
**Schema Version**: `1.2`
**Data Licence**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC BY 4.0`)

This human-readable register catalogues every legal instrument, master plan zoning regulation, amendment notification, enabling state statute, executive order, national reference standard, and boundary/map layer governing building construction in **Bengaluru South City Corporation** (constituted under the *Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024* by final notification dated 2 September 2025).

### Municipal Bye-laws Amendment Chain (`2004` → `2026`)
1. **`bbl-2003` (`2004-06-05`)**: Base municipal building bye-laws (Parts I–IV, Schedules I–IX, Tables 1–12).
2. **`bscc-bbl-amend-2026-draft` (`2026-04-01`, `superseded`)**: Public consultation draft proposing revised compounding/deviation limits and omission of ground rent bye-laws.
3. **`bscc-bbl-amend-2026` (`2026-05-14`, `in_force`)**: Final notification amending Bye-law `6.0` (inserting revised `Table 2B` permissible deviation tolerances), omitting ground-rent Bye-laws `3.8`, `3.9`, and `3.10`, and aligning municipal authority references with Bengaluru South City Corporation under the *Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024*.
