# Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) — Academic Partner Template

> **Note**: This is a plain-language, non-exclusive, no-fee academic partnership template for review and adaptation by `[Owner's Organisation]` and the partner college.

---

**MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING**

This Memorandum of Understanding ("**MoU**") is entered into on **`[Date]`** between:

1. **`[Owner's Organisation]`**, steward of the open-source **Bylaw One** project (`https://bylaws-india.vercel.app`), having its office at `[Address]` (hereinafter referred to as "**Bylaw One**"); and
2. **`[Name of College / University / Institution]`**, Department of `[Architecture / Urban Planning / Civil Engineering / Law]`, located at `[Address]` (hereinafter referred to as the "**Partner Institution**").

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this MoU is to establish a non-exclusive academic collaboration to verify, maintain, and teach open, page-traced municipal building regulations and master plan zoning data through **Bylaw One**, for the benefit of students, researchers, practitioners, and the public.

## 2. Joint Activities Each Semester

During each academic semester of the term, Bylaw One and the Partner Institution intend to collaborate on:

1. **One-Day Workshop**: Co-hosting a one-day hands-on workshop (`docs/partners/workshop-kit.md`) for students on reading statutory building codes, verifying structured records against official Gazette pages, and using AI tools with page citations.
2. **Faculty Session**: Holding a faculty review session to examine statutory resolution rules, curriculum integration, and local regulatory gaps.
3. **Supervised Verification Internships**: Offering 60 to 120-hour supervised student internships (`docs/partners/internship.md`) where students audit source registers, provisions, parameter tables, and georeferenced land-use maps.
4. **State / City Stewardship (Optional)**: Where agreed, serving as an academic steward for the Partner Institution's city or state within the Bylaw One Advisory Network.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Bylaw One Shall:
- Provide the open-source platform, verification task sets (`/verify/<task-set>`), workshop kit, and technical mentorship.
- Review and import completed verification files (`bylaws verify import`) under the two-independent-match quality rule.
- Credit participating students and faculty by name and institution on the website, in the Frictionless Data Package (`datapackage.json`), and in release notes.
- Issue certificates of completion to workshop participants and interns who complete their assigned verification sets.

### 3.2 The Partner Institution Shall:
- Nominate a **Faculty Coordinator** as the point of contact.
- Provide a classroom/studio venue and scheduling for the semester workshop and faculty session.
- Co-supervise student verification interns and verify their institutional affiliation.

## 4. Open Licences & Permanent Openness

1. **Code Licence**: All software and pipeline code in Bylaw One is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0 (`Apache-2.0`)**.
2. **Data Licence**: All structured regulatory datasets, parameter records, georeferenced map layers, overrides, and verification records are published under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC BY 4.0`)**.
3. **Permanent Openness**: Everything released under this collaboration stays open permanently. Neither party shall place published releases behind a paywall or assert exclusive rights over the shared datasets.

## 5. Named Credit & Citation

Every student and faculty member whose verification work is published in a Bylaw One release shall be credited by name and institution (`"Verified by [Name], [Institution], [Date]"`) in the release metadata and release notes. The Partner Institution may cite the dataset in academic publications using the project's `CITATION.cff` and Zenodo DOI metadata.

## 6. No Financial Commitment & Non-Exclusivity

- **No Fees**: This MoU involves no transfer of funds or financial obligation by either party. Any event expenses (such as refreshments or printed sheets) shall be borne by the party incurring them unless separately agreed in writing.
- **Non-Exclusive**: This MoU is non-exclusive. Both parties are free to partner with other universities, research bodies, professional institutes, and civic groups.

## 7. Term, Renewal, and Exit

- **Term**: This MoU takes effect on the date of signature and remains in effect for **two (2) years**, renewable by mutual written agreement (including by email).
- **Exit**: Either party may terminate this MoU at any time by giving **thirty (30) days' written notice** (including by email) to the other party. Termination shall not affect the permanent open licence (`CC BY 4.0` / `Apache-2.0`) or named credits of work already published.
