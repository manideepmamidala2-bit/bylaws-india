## 1. What Bylaw One Is

The rules that govern what can be built on a plot in an Indian city sit across municipal building bye-laws, master plan zoning regulations, dated gazette amendments, corporation notifications, and state legislation. **Bylaw One** brings those official publications together into one open, page-traced building code — starting with the **Bengaluru Pilot** (29 registered sources, 16 in-force instruments, 5,341 provisions, 271 active parameter rules, 5 City Corporations, 369 wards, and 1,276 georeferenced land-use zone polygons).

Every provision and table value on Bylaw One links to the exact page of the government PDF and an Internet Archive snapshot. All source code is published under **Apache-2.0** and all structured datasets under **CC BY 4.0**. Everything released stays open permanently.

## 2. What a Partner College Does Each Semester

Under a **free, non-exclusive Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)** (`docs/partners/mou-template.md`), a partner college of architecture, urban planning, civil engineering, or law takes part in three practical academic activities each semester:

1. **One-Day Verification & Statutory Reading Workshop (`docs/partners/workshop-kit.md`)**:
   - A structured 5-session studio workshop where students learn how municipal bye-laws, master plans, and gazette amendments interact, verify live provisions and table cells against official PDFs using `/verify/<task-set>`, and test how AI tools behave when grounded in page-traced legal text.
2. **Faculty Review Session**:
   - A 90-minute session with studio and urban-law faculty to review statutory resolution steps, contested or ambiguous provisions, and studio teaching modules.
3. **Supervised Verification Internships (`docs/partners/internship.md`)**:
   - 60 to 120-hour supervised student internships verifying provisions, tables, and georeferenced land-use map sheets for the college's city or state.

## 3. What the College and Students Receive

- **Named Credit in Every Release**: Every student and faculty verifier whose work passes the two-match verification threshold is credited by name and institution on the website, in the Frictionless Data Package (`datapackage.json`), and in the release notes.
- **Citable Open Dataset**: Releases carry Zenodo/DOI citation metadata (`CITATION.cff`, `.zenodo.json`) suitable for studio briefs, dissertations, and urban research publications.
- **Certificates of Completion**: Workshop participants and verification interns receive signed certificates detailing the instruments and records they verified.
- **City or State Steward Role**: Partner colleges may serve as the academic steward for their city or state in the Bylaw One Partner & Advisory Network (`docs/partners/charter.md`).
- **Zero Financial Cost**: There are no fees, software licences, or exclusivity obligations.
