# Bylaw One — Five-Minute Live Demo Script

**Total time**: 5 minutes · **Starting URL**: `https://bylaws-india.vercel.app`

---

## Minute 1 (0:00 – 1:00) — The Home Story (`/`)

1. **Open `https://bylaws-india.vercel.app`**.
2. Point to the headline: **"Bengaluru's building rules. One code."** and the subline: *"16 official documents from 8 bodies, joined into one readable code. Every rule traced to its page. Current as of 14 May 2026."*
3. Scroll to **01 / The problem (`Many sources. One building.`)**: show the convergence illustration of in-force Acts, master plan regulations, building bye-laws, and 2025–2026 amendments joining into one code.
4. Scroll to **02 / Follow one rule (`How FAR came to be what it is today`)**:
   - Click through Steps 1 → 5 (`2004 Bye-laws` → `2007 Master Plan Table 10` → `2017 Bye-law 1.4` → `2025 Premium FAR` → `Today`).
   - Point out that every step shows the **verbatim quote** from the gazette and a **`View page`** link (`#page=N`) to the exact official PDF page.

---

## Minute 2 (1:00 – 2:00) — A Subject Page (`/j/in-ka-bengaluru/topics/setbacks`)

1. Click **The code** in the top navigation (or click **Setbacks** in `03 / The code, by subject`).
2. Point to **01 / Governing rules — What applies today**:
   - Show the clean table of governing setback rules (`12% of plot depth`, `8% of plot depth`, `8% of plot width` from the **5 January 2026** zoning amendment, `p. 2`, and `Table 9` for buildings above 12 m).
   - Point to the one-line applicability explanation: *"Buildings up to 12 m use Table 8; taller buildings use Table 9 (clauses 3.1(ii) and (iii), January 2026)."*
3. Expand **Older or replaced rules**: show how the 2007 Table 8 rows and the 2004 Building Bye-laws rows are preserved underneath with **"Replaced by Zoning amendment, 5 January 2026"** and **"Master plan rules apply instead (bye-law 1.4)"**.
4. Switch the corporation selector at the top to **South** (`/j/in-ka-bengaluru-south/topics/approvals-and-certificates`) to show how a City Corporation's **14 May 2026** amendment (`Bye-law 6.0(i)`) is badged **"In Bengaluru South City Corporation"**.

---

## Minute 3 (2:00 – 3:15) — The Map & Place Card (`/j/in-ka-bengaluru-south`)

1. Click **Map** in the top navigation (`/j/in-ka-bengaluru-south`).
2. Point out the honest coverage and accuracy lines at the top:
   - *"Land-use zones cover 1.5% of Greater Bengaluru, in parts of Bengaluru South."*
   - *"Zone boundaries are approximate: typically about 8 m from street centre-lines, and within about 21 m in 9 out of 10 places."*
3. In the **Search by place, neighbourhood, road, landmark, or ward** box, type **`Jayanagar`** and select **Jayanagar 4th Block**:
   - Watch the map fly to Jayanagar and update the shareable URL hash (`#lat,lng,zoom,place`).
   - Point to the **Place Card** on the right: it shows **Corporation** (`Bengaluru South City Corporation`), **Ward** (`Ward 8 · Tilak Nagara`), **Planning district** (`2.10 · Jayanagar`), and **Land-use zone** (`Residential (Main) (R-MAIN)`).
   - Scroll through **Rules for this zone** (`Uses`, `FAR`, `Ground coverage`, `Setbacks`, `Height`, `Parking`) with page links and the **Bye-law 1.4 verbatim quote on tap**.
4. Check **Original map sheets** in the layer switch and drag the **Sheet opacity** slider (`0–100%`) to show the original BDA RMP 2015 Proposed Land Use map aligned underneath the vector zones.
5. Mention **Browse by ward** (`/j/in-ka-bengaluru-south/wards`) for screen-reader and no-map access.

---

## Minute 4 (3:15 – 4:15) — Workshop Verification (`/verify/bengaluru-pilot-sample`)

1. Open **`/verify/bengaluru-pilot-sample`** (linked from `/checks` and `/join`).
2. Enter a name and college in **Your name and institution**.
3. On **Task 1**, show the provision/rule text on the left and click **Open official page (p. N) ↗** on the right.
4. Click **Match** and **Export completed CSV**:
   - Explain that answers are saved in the browser with zero server accounts, and that when workshop CSVs are imported via `bylaws verify import`, a record becomes **"Verified by a person"** only after **two independent Match verdicts**, crediting both students by name and college in the release.

---

## Minute 5 (4:15 – 5:00) — Use with AI & How to Join (`/use-with-ai` & `/join`)

1. Click **Use with AI** (`/use-with-ai`):
   - Show `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, the `.md` Markdown URL for any page (`/j/in-ka-bengaluru/topics/far.md`), and the static JSON API (`/api/v1/openapi.json`).
   - Highlight the four honesty rules every AI must follow (cite the page, label derived values, ask for missing plot details, confirm with the authority).
2. Click **Join** (`/join`):
   - Close by inviting colleges (free semester MoU, 1-day workshop, 60–120 hour internships), students, practitioners, and civic groups to join the Bengaluru pilot.
