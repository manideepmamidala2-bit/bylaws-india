## Underlying Regulatory Texts

The underlying building bye-laws, master plan zoning regulations, gazette notifications, and official maps are **public government publications** issued by Indian municipal corporations, urban development authorities, and state governments (Section 52(1)(q) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957). Our CC BY 4.0 license covers the curation, structural extraction, normalization, cross-reference linking, georeferencing, and verification metadata produced by Bylaw One, and does not assert copyright over the underlying official statutes or government notifications.
