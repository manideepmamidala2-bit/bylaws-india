## Underlying Regulatory Texts

The underlying building bye-laws, master plan zoning regulations, gazette notifications, and official maps are **government publications** issued by Indian municipal corporations, urban development authorities, and state governments. Our CC BY 4.0 claim covers the curation, structural extraction, normalization, cross-reference linking, georeferencing, and verification metadata produced by this project, and does not assert copyright over the underlying official statutes or government notifications.
